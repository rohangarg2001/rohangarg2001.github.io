Stanford University · Mechanical Engineering

I work on navigation and loco-manipulation for legged robots with Prof. Marco Pavone at Stanford. My research focuses on semantic exploration, GPU-native graph representations, and reinforcement learning for autonomous robots.

I studied mechanical engineering at Stanford (M.S., Robotics Specialization) and IIT Delhi (B.Tech.). Previously, I worked on autonomy at Field AI and drone safety with Prof. Somil Bansal.

Selected project

STANFORD × BERKELEYTIAMATDARPA CHALLENGE · 2ND PLACE

DARPA TIAMAT · GoldenGait

Stanford–Berkeley collaboration · 2026

Developed autonomy for semantic exploration on legged robots as part of the team's second-place DARPA TIAMAT submission.

My work includes GPU-native navigation graphs and GNN exploration policies trained with RL that outperform state-of-the-art exploration planners by 20%. I am also developing agentic and RL loco-manipulation policies to identify and move semantically appropriate objects.
